3D model

Futuristic scythe

A low-poly sci-fi scythe built around a long, angular handle and an oversized curved blade. Dark surfaces contrast with pale metallic fittings and a vivid pink cutting edge.

About the model

This stylized scythe pairs a long, tapered dark shaft with a broad blade that sweeps outward into a sharp point. Angular shaping, pale metallic fasteners, and a bright pink blade edge give the silhouette a futuristic feel while keeping its low-poly form clear.
